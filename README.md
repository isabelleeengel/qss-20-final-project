Inequities in Women's Healthcare
QSS 20 Final Project: investigating racial and geographic disparities in maternal mortality across the United States.

Project Overview:
This project examines whether disparities in U.S. maternal mortality compound across two dimensions, race and state lines
Using the CDC wonder data, I ask the following questions
1) Do racial disparities in maternal mortality remain consistent across all states, or are they concentrated in specific states?
2) Are there states where the racial gap narrows or disappears? (suggesting policy differences that impact the quality of women's healthcare in those regions)

Data: from CDC Wonder data 
Limitations: CDC suppresses data with death counts under 10 which limits the representations of some racial groups
